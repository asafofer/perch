Perch, round two

The first version missed. Here is what went wrong and what I'd do instead. Pick a character, play with the card, and tell me what you feel.

The bird looked cheapDrawn from ovals and triangles, it had no personality. I'm replacing it with characters built around motion, not illustration.
The notch has no pixelsThe hardware notch is a hole, so nothing can live inside it. The character can only peek out from behind it or sit beside it.
The card read like a formSmall capital labels and a dense grid. The new card says one thing in plain words, then offers one action.

1. The character

All three peek out from behind the notch. Switch the mood to see how each one behaves.

A. Peeker

Just two eyes. Sleepy lines, wide awake, then happy arcs. Minimal, hard to get wrong, and it can look toward whatever it is reading.

B. Pip

A soft blob that squashes and stretches. It bobs when awake and jumps when happy. The most playful and the most Clicky-like.

C. Ember

A small glowing light. It breathes when idle, brightens when awake, and throws sparks when happy. Calm and premium, with no face.

2. The card

The text sits beside the notch where the screen actually has pixels. Tab, Return and Esc work as they will in the app.
