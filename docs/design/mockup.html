What it reads

Your Claude Code sessions, the git branch, and your window layout. You never type a note.

When it appears

When you unlock your Mac, or open a project you left more than 20 minutes ago. Otherwise the bird sleeps.

What it asks for

Folder access to read sessions and git, and Accessibility to put windows back. Nothing else.
