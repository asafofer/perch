Grok Bot, Muse, Oh My Pi and Dots

- Grok Bot: each bot is a row wearing its own avatar, and you answer its questions and reply from the notch
- Muse Code in any terminal: its sessions are listed with live status, and you can reply and pick up from the notch
- Oh My Pi works like pi, and Perch answers its questions from the notch
- Dots (ChatGPT Pro, beta): each dot is a row, and Pick up opens it
- Settings and setup group your agents by kind, and you can switch off a terminal like tmux to hide its sessions
- Several Oh My Pi or Muse sessions in one folder each go back to the right pane
- Click the character for a different reaction each time
