Smoother lists, and questions in full

- A question in the notch is shown in full, and hovering a choice shows its title, its note and whether it is recommended
- Hovering a session in the list selects it, with a hand cursor, and the highlight follows the pointer smoothly without scrolling by itself
- The arrow keys no longer jump back to where the mouse rests, in Settings and in the list
- Command+comma closes Settings, the same shortcut that opens it
- Settings: "Away from your Mac for" is now "Welcome back after"
