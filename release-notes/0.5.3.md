Herdr's app and your character at the notch

- Herdr's desktop app: its sessions wear Herdr's icon, and you can pick up, reply and reopen them from the notch
- New session asks where a terminal agent opens: Terminal, Ghostty, cmux, agterm or Herdr
- Character at the notch (Settings, Notifications): your character waits in the notch when an agent asks, and gets impatient
- Grok Bot shows a bot at work as working, and a choice you pick is pressed without bringing Grok Bot forward
- Question cards no longer flicker while they wait
- Finer, rounder icons throughout
