Rename, archive or delete a session from the notch

- More (Command+K) on a card and in the list: rename, archive or delete a session, as far as its agent allows
- Works for the Claude app, Claude Code in a terminal, Codex, OpenCode and pi
- Welcome back after can be Off (Settings, Notifications): the card then opens only when you hover or ask
- The list shows the New session shortcut you set, not Option+Command+N
- With Grok Bot or Dots in front, the notch no longer speaks up about their bots
- A card's footer reads Back, Reply, Preview, Open
