Pull requests, Cursor and agterm

- Sessions show their pull requests: green open, purple merged, red closed, orange when one needs you. Hover for details, click to open
- Turn on Checks and a pull request with failing checks, asked-for changes or a conflict puts its session under Needs you
- Cursor chats show up beside your other sessions (beta): Pick up the exact chat, reply, and answer Allow or Deny from the notch
- Works with agterm like cmux: Pick up goes to the exact session and pane, and replies are typed into it (thanks @jeniasaigak)
