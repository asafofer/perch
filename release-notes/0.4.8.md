Claude tasks and chats in Perch

- Your Claude app tasks show up beside your coding sessions: working, waiting for your OK, or finished, with what Claude found
- Turn on Claude chats in Settings to see Claude's last reply, and answer from the notch
- Choosing your apps uses simple checks, with Claude as one tile for Code, Tasks and Chats
- Press R right after sending and the reply opens on your message, with the step the agent is on below it
- A reply to a terminal session goes only into its own tab. When Perch isn't sure which tab, it copies the reply instead
- Warp sessions open on their own tab at once, with no tab flicking (thanks @stolery)
