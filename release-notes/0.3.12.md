Start a session from the notch, and copy what the agent said

- Command+Option+N, or New session in the list, starts a session in Claude, Codex or your terminal while you stay where you were
- What the agent said, on the card or in a reply, can be selected and copied with Command+C
- A card with words selected stays open, so you can copy at your own pace
- After an update, a What's new card says what changed
- Highlights in the list and in Settings follow the pointer and never stay lit after it leaves
