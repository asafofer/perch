Things that snapped now move

- A card's status changes in place: the words swap and the spinner shrinks away
- The list's highlight slides from row to row, and rows glide when the list sorts itself
- Moving to the next session's card cross-fades only what changed
- Questions on a card: each check draws itself in, and only the number in "Question 1 of 3" changes
- Settings menus grow out of their button, and switches settle with a small bounce
- The gear and close buttons light up the moment you point at them
