Choose which screen Perch opens on

- Show Perch on in Settings: the screen with the mouse, the screen with the active window, or one display by name
- A chosen display that is unplugged stays in the menu, greyed, and Perch follows the mouse until it is back
- Hover opens on external displays now lives in the Show Perch on menu
- On a display without a notch, the card and the live activity close by shrinking to nothing at the top centre
- A failed update says why and what to do (move Perch to Applications, check your connection), and a Mac that cannot run the newest version is told so
- Settings has more room between its rows
